In this Electrum Best Seller, explore the inequities of a caste-based society in which a bandit murders innocent merchants and gives away their wealth to help the poor. Is he a villain, an agent of justice, or something in between? The party must wade through political intrigue and a dangerous jungle to discover the truth before social tensions rip their society apart.
