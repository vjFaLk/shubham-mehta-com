**'A Sticky Situation'** is a heist-style adventure written for Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition. The game is set in the mystical town of Mahaburg where a divine pot of 'Amrut', the nectar of the gods, lies guarded by the Wildmother's followers; waiting for someone smart enough to steal it. 

Inspired by **Indian mythology and folklore**, the adventure presents a unique set of monsters, items, characters, and culture for players to interact with as they take on this stealth orientated challenge.
